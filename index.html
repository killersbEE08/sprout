FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account to use Sprout?

No. Sprout is guest-friendly — you can create and track habits without signing up. An account is only needed if you want cloud sync across devices or access to the community.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sprout is offline-first. All your data is stored on your device, so the app works fully with no network connection. Reminders also fire locally, even offline.

Are the streaks and stats accurate?

Every number is derived from your actual completion records. Streaks are calculated in calendar-day space and stored in UTC, so daylight saving and travel never corrupt your progress.

How do I delete my data or account?

You can reset your data or delete your account directly in the app's settings. You can also request full account deletion by email — see our account deletion page for details.

Is Sprout really free?

Yes. The core habit tracker is free forever. Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks advanced features and helps support ongoing development.
