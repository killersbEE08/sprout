FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account to use Sprout?

No. Sprout is account-free — there's no login or email screen. You start practising right away and your progress is saved on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Sprout is offline-first. All your progress, stats and settings are stored on your device, so the app works fully with no network connection.

Who is Sprout for?

It's built for learners aged 13 to 60 who want sharper practical maths. A short placement assessment sets your starting level, and difficulty adapts to how you answer.

Are the stats and streaks accurate?

Every number — accuracy, response time, XP, streaks and skill mastery — comes from your real answers. Wrong answers are saved in the Mistake Notebook with the correct method so you can review them later.

Is Sprout really free?

Yes. The core game is free, including most skill games and 30- and 60-second Rapid Fire rounds. Premium is an optional subscription that unlocks Expert difficulty, longer rounds, Smart Workout and detailed diagnostics.
