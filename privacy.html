Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Sprout ("we", "us") operates the Sprout habit & wellness mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Sprout is offline-first: you can use the core app with no account and no data leaving your device.

1. Information we collect

  • On-device data — habits, tasks, goals, routines, meditation sessions and their completion history are stored locally on your device. If you never create an account, this data stays on your device.
  • Account information — when you sign up we store your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your name and profile photo. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Community content — posts, comments, likes and reports you submit. You appear publicly under a pseudonymous @handle, never your real name or email.
  • Uploaded images — photos you attach to community posts.
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and usage data to keep the App reliable (only if analytics/crash reporting is enabled).

We do not sell your personal data.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App and, if you have an account, sync your habit data across your devices.
  • To operate community features (feed, likes, comments) and moderation.
  • To send in-app and (if enabled) push notifications you have not disabled.
  • To display clearly-labelled sponsored content in the community feed.
  • To detect, prevent and address abuse, spam or policy violations.

3. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. EEA/UK)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the App), for our legitimate interests (security, moderation, product improvement), and with your consent (e.g. optional notifications/analytics). You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

4. Sharing & processors

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and, if enabled, Analytics/Crashlytics/Cloud Messaging) to run the cloud features of the App. Their processing is governed by Google's terms. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your content in-app, reset all local data from Settings, and request full account deletion. See our dedicated account deletion page for step-by-step instructions. We will remove your personal data within 30 days of a verified request, except where retention is legally required.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's infrastructure. Access to private data is restricted by security rules so only you (and, for public posts, the community) can read it.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Contact support@sprout.app to exercise them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; we will post the new date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App.
