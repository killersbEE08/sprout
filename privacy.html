Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Sprout ("we", "us", "our") is developed and published by one1 Eleven dev, the data controller responsible for the Sprout maths training mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Sprout is offline-first and account-free: there is no login or email screen, and your practice data stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

  • On-device data — your practice progress, XP, levels, streaks, accuracy and response-time stats, Mistake Notebook entries and app settings are stored locally on your device. Because Sprout is account-free, this data stays on your device.
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and usage data to keep the App reliable (only if analytics/crash reporting is enabled). This is not linked to your identity.

We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App and save your practice progress and settings on your device.
  • To keep the App stable and improve it using aggregated, de-identified diagnostics (if enabled).
  • To detect, prevent and address crashes, abuse or technical problems.

3. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. EEA/UK)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing the App), for our legitimate interests (security, moderation, product improvement), and with your consent (e.g. optional notifications/analytics). You may withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

4. Sharing & processors

If diagnostics are enabled, we use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to collect aggregated crash and usage data that helps us keep the App stable. Their processing is governed by Google's terms. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your practice data stays on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can reset all data from Settings at any time, and uninstalling the App removes it. See our dedicated data deletion page for step-by-step instructions. Any de-identified diagnostics are retained only in aggregate.

6. Security

Your practice data is stored locally on your device. Any diagnostics sent to Google Firebase are encrypted in transit and handled on Google's infrastructure. We keep the data we touch to the minimum needed to run and improve the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age.

8. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Contact hello@princelabs.me to exercise them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; we will post the new date above and, for material changes, notify you in the App.
