🗑️ Account & data

Delete your account

Remove your Sprout account and associated data at any time.

You are always in control of your data. This page explains exactly what gets deleted and the two ways to do it — directly in the app, or by email request. This process applies to the Sprout app published by Prince Labs.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

Open Settings

Launch Sprout and tap the Profile tab, then open Settings.

Choose "Delete account"

Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete account. (Guests without an account can use Reset all data to wipe local data instead.)

Confirm

Confirm the prompt. Your account and cloud data are permanently removed, and locally stored habits are cleared from the device.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll handle it for you.

Email: support@sprout.app
Subject: Account deletion request
Include the email address linked to your Sprout account so we can verify the request.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account and authentication record (email / Google Sign-In link).
  • Your habits, tasks, goals, routines, meditation sessions and completion history.
  • Your community content — posts, comments, likes and your @handle.
  • Images you uploaded to community posts.
  • Habit data stored locally on your device (cleared when you delete in-app).

What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of information where legally required, or in aggregated/anonymised form that can no longer identify you (for example, abuse-prevention and moderation records). Anonymised statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be kept.

This can't be undone. Account deletion is permanent. If you only want to step away, you can simply sign out — your data stays until you choose to delete it.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is effective immediately for your account and local data. For email requests, we complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your identity, and usually much sooner.
