Your data

Delete your data

Reset or remove your Sprout data at any time.

You are always in control of your data. Sprout is account-free, so everything you create lives on your device. This page explains exactly what gets removed and the two ways to do it — directly in the app, or by email request. This applies to the Sprout app, developed and published by one1 Eleven dev.

Option 1 — Reset in the app (fastest)

Open Settings

Launch Sprout and tap the Profile tab, then open Settings.

Choose "Reset all data"

Scroll to the Data section and tap Reset all data.

Confirm

Confirm the prompt. Your progress, stats and settings are permanently cleared from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, or you want any diagnostics associated with your device removed, email us and we'll handle it.

Email: hello@princelabs.me
Subject: Data deletion request
Tell us your device model so we can locate any diagnostics tied to it.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your practice progress, XP, levels and streaks.
  • Your accuracy, response-time and skill-mastery statistics.
  • Your Mistake Notebook entries and scheduled reviews.
  • Your app preferences (nickname, theme, text size and other settings).
  • All of this is stored locally and is cleared when you reset in-app or uninstall.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of information where legally required, or in aggregated/anonymised form that can no longer identify you (for example, de-identified crash and usage diagnostics). Anonymised statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be kept.

This can't be undone. Resetting your data is permanent. If you only want to pause, simply close the app — your data stays until you choose to reset it.

How long it takes

In-app reset is effective immediately. For email requests covering device diagnostics, we complete removal within 30 days of verifying the request, and usually much sooner.
