Frequently asked questions

How do I back up or sync my data?

Create a free account and sign in to enable cloud sync across your devices. Without an account, your data stays safely on your device.

My streak looks wrong after travelling — why?

It shouldn't. Sprout stores completions in UTC and calculates streaks in calendar-day space, so time zones and daylight saving don't break your streak. If something looks off, email us with details.

Reminders aren't showing up

Make sure notifications are enabled for Sprout in your device settings, and that Do Not Disturb / battery optimisation isn't blocking the app. Reminders are delivered on-device, so they work offline.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Premium subscriptions are billed through your app store. Manage or cancel anytime from your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Account, or by emailing us. Full instructions are on the account deletion page.

Still stuck? Email support@sprout.app and include your device model and app version — it helps us help you faster.
